Two thresholds, two jobs

Every cell below carries two similarity thresholds, and confusing them ships the wrong one.

yardstick

The TAR@FAR threshold is set by pinning every encoder to the same false-accept budget ({{ '%g'|format(primary_far|float * 100) }}%), so the comparison is fair. It is a measuring instrument for ranking encoders. It is never deployed.

deployment

The max-F1 threshold is the cut-off that best balances precision and recall on the real score distributions. This is the one stored in the gallery and used live.
