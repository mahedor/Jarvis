What was locked in

{{ '%.2f'|format(detection_threshold) }} confidence, from where F1 peaks on the two hard datasets. FDDB's optimum sits much higher because nearly everything in it is easy to be confident about; folding that in would bias the threshold upward and start dropping exactly the distant, profile and backlit faces a household camera actually produces.

Erring low is the right direction here: a missed face is a person JARVIS fails to greet, while a false detection costs one wasted embedding that the recognition stage rejects at its own threshold anyway.
